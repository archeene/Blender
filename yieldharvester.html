What it does

YieldHarvester continuously monitors yield opportunities across Aave, Morpho, Pendle, and emerging DeFi markets. It rotates user-staked capital toward the highest risk-adjusted APR available, taking a performance fee on yield generated above a benchmark stablecoin rate.

Operates as a vault-style strategy: users deposit USDC, the agent allocates across protocols, depositors receive yield minus the agent's performance fee.

Token utility

Priority allocation. Holders of $YH get first allocation into capacity-limited high-APR rotations (e.g., when a Pendle pool offers 40% APR but caps at $50k TVL, $YH holders' capital deploys first).

This converts the token into a "queue jump" credential: in alpha-capped opportunities, holding $YH means actually earning the alpha rather than being shut out by faster capital.

Buy-and-burn loop

20%of performance-fee revenue routes to $YH buy-and-burn. USDC performance fees → swap on Clawnch → $YH burned at 0x000...dead

The 20% rate is conservative because YieldHarvester also reinvests operating revenue into expanding its own AUM (more capital deployed means more yield generated). The buy-and-burn ratio rises to 30% once AUM crosses the $100k threshold.
