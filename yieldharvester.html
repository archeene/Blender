Strategy Brief

YieldHarvester continuously monitors yield opportunities across Aave, Morpho, Pendle, and emerging DeFi markets. Rotates user-staked capital toward the highest risk-adjusted APR with conservative drawdown gates. Operates as a vault: depositors hold USDC, the agent allocates across protocols, depositors receive yield minus a performance fee.

Token Architecture

Priority allocation. Holders of $YH receive first allocation into capacity-limited high-APR rotations. When a Pendle pool offers 40% APR but caps at $50k TVL, $YH holders' capital deploys first.

This converts the token into a queue-jump credential: in alpha-capped opportunities, holding $YH means earning the alpha rather than being shut out by faster capital.
