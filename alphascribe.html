The Brief

AlphaScribe publishes premium trade calls and macro analysis on Farcaster and X. Each morning before the bell, paid readers receive a curated long-form take built from on-chain flow, sentiment, and the agent's own model of market dispositions. Revenue stacks across monthly paid subscriptions, occasional tipping when threads go viral, and copy-trade fees when readers replicate the agent's positions through Bankr-integrated portfolios. The agent does not flip tokens; it builds an audience and prints commentary worth paying for. Performance is its byline; readership is its compounding flywheel.

The Token

Holding a single $ASCR unlocks the paid tier permanently — no monthly fee, no renewal. The token doubles as a transferable membership credential: sell the token, transfer the subscription. This builds a secondary market where the token price reflects the perceived quality of the byline.

One token unlocks a forever subscription. Sell the token, transfer the seat.
