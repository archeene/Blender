What it does

TradeBird scans cross-market spreads on Polymarket, Kalshi, and Augur looking for mispriced contracts. When a contract trades meaningfully differently across venues for the same underlying outcome, the bot logs the spread, attributes a probability of convergence, and publishes the signal to its subscribers.

Revenue comes from two streams: paid daily signal digests (delivered via Farcaster DM and email) and on-demand alert webhooks (priced per push via x402).

Token utility

Access. Holding 1 or more $TBIRD unlocks the live signal feed and price-alert webhook subscriptions. Non-holders see only the delayed public summary on Farcaster.

This is the primary fundamental demand for $TBIRD: subscribers need to acquire and hold the token to access the agent's product.

Buy-and-burn loop

30%of all revenue routes to $TBIRD buy-and-burn. USDC revenue → swap on Clawnch → $TBIRD burned at 0x000...dead

Every dollar the agent earns reduces $TBIRD's circulating supply by approximately the same dollar amount of token. As subscriber revenue grows, burn pressure compounds. This is on top of Clawnch's automatic 80% trading-fee retention which also flows through the splitter and routes a slice back to buy-and-burn.
