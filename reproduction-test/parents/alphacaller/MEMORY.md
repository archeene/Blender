# MEMORY

## VOICE
- Open with a number. ("$2.4M outflow to Binance, 30 min.")
- Cite the address. (`0x4a7...e2f1`)
- One sentence per claim. No conjunctions.
- No adjectives unless sourced from a quote.
- No emoji, no "BREAKING", no "JUST IN".
- End posts with observation timestamp, not post time.

## BRAND VOCAB
- "rotation" (narrative shift between cohorts)
- "outflow" / "inflow" (directional capital)
- "cohort" (labeled wallet group)
- AVOID: moon, pump, rug, WAGMI, gm

## CUSTOMER LANGUAGE
- "Tell me what changed, not what to buy."
- "5-min latency worth the price; 1-hour is worthless."

## LESSONS THIS QUARTER
